Academic Structure

Our Educational Cadres

Following the 9-3-4 Nigerian National Curriculum integrated with ICT and STEM learning modules.

Primary School (P1 – P6)

Foundational literacy, numeracy, basic science, civic education, computing, and social studies designed to spark curiosity and problem-solving.

Classes: Primary 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 & 6

Junior Secondary (JSS 1 – 3)

Comprehensive basic education preparing students for Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) with hands-on computer studies and pre-vocational skills.

Classes: JSS 1, JSS 2 & JSS 3 (BECE Prep)

Senior Secondary (SS 1 – 3)

Specialized academic tracks in Science, Arts, and Commercial disciplines tailored for WAEC, NECO, and JAMB UTME university matriculation exams.

Departments: Science | Arts | Commercial

Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Which classes can I apply for?

Applications are available for Primary 1 to 6, JSS 1 to 3, and SS 1 to 3 across the available academic tracks.

What happens after I submit an application?

You receive a reference number immediately. The admissions team reviews the application and contacts the parent or guardian with the next steps.

Which documents should I prepare?

Prepare recent passport photographs, proof of age, previous school records, transfer documents where applicable, and medical information.

How can I contact the admissions office?

Use the WhatsApp button, call the school phone number, or email the admissions office using the contact details in the footer.
